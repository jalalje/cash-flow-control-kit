# Payment verification checklist

Use this checklist whenever a customer, vendor, or employee asks for a payment change, new bank account, beneficiary change, or new payment destination.

## Required steps

1. Pause the payment or transaction.
2. Do not respond directly using the request details alone.
3. Find the trusted contact information from a previously verified record.
4. Call or message the known trusted contact using the previous verified channel.
5. Confirm the change verbally and record the time and method of confirmation.
6. Require a second approval if the amount is material or the request is unusual.
7. Check whether the account or payment details match historical records.
8. Compare the request with the original invoice, contract, or purchase order.
9. Record evidence in a payment-risk log.
10. Do not bypass internal authorization policies.

## Red flags

- urgent language with threats or secrecy
- a bank account or beneficiary that differs from historical records
- request arrives by email or message from a lookalike domain
- unusual amount or short notice
- request to send funds to a new location outside the usual process
- request to avoid normal approval or verification controls

## Important note

This checklist helps reduce risk and documents a human review process. It does not guarantee that a payment request is legitimate. Always follow your bank and internal policy requirements.
