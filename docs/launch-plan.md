# Launch plan

## Phase 1: product validation

- Publish a landing page and simple calculator
- Offer an early product at a low entry price
- Collect email sign-ups and first buyers
- Ask buyers to report the three features they use most

## Phase 2: template refinement

- Improve the workbook based on actual user feedback
- Add sample scenarios for agencies, consultants, and freelancers
- Simplify category names to match real-world businesses

## Phase 3: premium add-ons

- setup help
- custom categories for a business
- one-on-one onboarding
- small-team workflow support

## Positioning note

The strongest positioning is not 'AI finance guru' or 'AI investing adviser.'
The safer and more credible positioning is:

> Practical cash-flow and payment-risk operations support for small businesses.

That message is aligned with real financial pain points, easier to validate, and less risky from a compliance perspective.
