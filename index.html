FAQ

Is this a fintech app?

Is this meant to replace an accountant?

No. It is a planning and operations aid for better visibility and process discipline.

Does it guarantee fraud detection?

No. It helps identify risk and documents a verification process, but important decisions still require human review.

Who is this for?

Freelancers, agencies, consultants, and small service businesses with irregular income, invoice cycles, and payment risk.
