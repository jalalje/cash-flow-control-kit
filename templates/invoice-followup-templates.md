# Invoice follow-up templates

## 1. Reminder before due date

Subject: Invoice [number] due on [date]

Hi [Client Name],

This is a quick reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] is due on [date].

Please let me know if your accounts payable team requires any additional information from me.

Thank you for your business.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## 2. One-day overdue follow-up

Subject: Payment status for invoice [number]

Hi [Client Name],

Invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [date]. Could you confirm the expected payment date or let me know if there is a blocker on your end?

If there is anything I can provide to support approval, please let me know.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## 3. 7-day overdue follow-up

Subject: Action required for invoice [number]

Hi [Client Name],

Invoice [number] for [amount] remains outstanding and is now [X] days overdue.

Please arrange payment by [date] or confirm any issue that requires resolution.

If your payment process requires a PO number or additional documentation, send it and I will help resolve it quickly.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## 4. Final escalation message

Subject: Final reminder – invoice [number]

Hi [Client Name],

This is a final reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] has not yet been paid and is now [X] days overdue.

Please confirm the expected payment date or contact me directly so we can resolve any issue.

Best,
[Your Name]
